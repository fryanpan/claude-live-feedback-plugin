retry-policy.md3

Retry policy

Requests are retried up to three times with exponential backoff. The retry loop swallows the underlying error, so a caller sees only the final timeout.

The timeout is applied per attempt rather than across the whole operation, which means a slow endpoint can still exceed the budget.
